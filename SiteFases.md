# BijHommels Website Project Plan

## Project Overview

### Purpose

The website serves as the online presence of **BijHommels**. Its primary goals are to:

- Introduce who I am
- Explain what BijHommels offers
- Display tutoring prices
- Make it easy for visitors to contact me
- Encourage visitors to book a tutoring session

---

## Target Audience

The website is intended for:

- Parents looking for tutoring for their children

---

## Technology Stack

### Website Type

- Static website
- Hand-coded HTML, CSS, and JavaScript

### Hosting

- GitHub Pages

### Domain

- BijHommels.nl

---

# Development Roadmap

## Phase 1 - Requirements

### Goals

- Define the purpose of the website.
- Make it immediately clear that BijHommels offers tutoring.
- Show the subjects and pricing clearly.
- Make contact easy through WhatsApp.
- Encourage parents to book a first tutoring session.

### Purpose

BijHommels.nl is the online presence for a small tutoring startup. The website should
explain what BijHommels offers, who the tutoring is for, what it costs, and how parents
can get in touch.

### Target Audience

The main audience is parents looking for tutoring for their children in secondary school.
The tone should be clear, trustworthy, personal, and easy to understand.

### Technology Stack

- Static website
- Hand-coded HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
- Hosted with GitHub Pages
- Custom domain: BijHommels.nl

### Initial Scope

The first release is a single-page website. It does not need accounts, a booking system,
payments, a backend, or a blog. The most important conversion is sending a WhatsApp
message.

---

## Phase 2 - Homepage Planning

The homepage will consist of the following sections:

1. Header
2. Hero
3. About Me
4. Subjects
5. How It Works
6. Pricing
7. Why Choose BijHommels?
8. Frequently Asked Questions
9. Contact
10. Footer

### Header

The header should contain the BijHommels logo and simple navigation links. It should
stay compact and work well on mobile.

### Hero

The first screen should quickly explain the offer:

> Bijles voor middelbare scholieren in wiskunde, natuurkunde en scheikunde.

Primary action: send a WhatsApp message.

### About Me

Koen Hommels provides tutoring mainly for upper secondary students. For lower secondary math tutoring, help may also be available through his sister.

### Subjects

The first version should list:

- Wiskunde A
- Wiskunde B
- Natuurkunde
- Scheikunde

### How It Works

1. Parent or student sends a WhatsApp message.
2. A first lesson is planned.
3. The first lesson works as a paid trial lesson.
4. After that, follow-up tutoring is planned if it is a good fit.

### Pricing

- 30 euro per hour
- Standard tutoring session: 2 hours
- Exceptions can be agreed in advance

### Why Choose BijHommels?

This section should focus on:

- Personal explanation
- Calm step-by-step tutoring
- Focus on exact subjects
- Easy contact through WhatsApp

### Frequently Asked Questions

Possible questions:

- Is the first lesson free?
- How long is one tutoring session?
- Which subjects are available?
- Is tutoring possible for lower secondary students?

### Contact

Main contact method:

- WhatsApp: +31 6 38323500

## Phase 3 - Branding

### Brand Direction

The visual style should feel like opening a math workbook or notebook. The site should
feel personal and handmade, but still clear and professional.

### Logo

The current logo file is:

- `ChatGPTLogo.png`

The final logo may be replaced later when Nienke makes the finished version.

### Brand Colors

```css
--bijyellow: #f5c542;
--bijred: #b63a32;
--bijdark: #222222;
--bijgrey: #666666;
--bijlight: #f8f6f0;
```

### Style Keywords

- Math workbook
- Pencil notes
- Paper texture
- Clear blocks
- Friendly tutoring
- Calm, personal, reliable

### Typography Direction

Use simple readable fonts for now. Later, a handwritten accent font could be added for
small labels or notes, but body text should stay very readable.

### Current Design Choices

- Light paper-colored background
- Dark outlines
- Yellow highlights
- Red accent labels
- Card-like sections inspired by notebook pages
